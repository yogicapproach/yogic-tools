Privacy

SYP Companion · last updated 9 November 2026 · describes version 0.11.x

The extension sends nothing about you to us. It has no analytics, no telemetry, no accounts and no advertising, and we have no server that could receive anything you read, search for or click. A few lists the extension keeps for you (below) stay in your browser, on your device, and nowhere else.

What it stores, and where

Everything below is kept in your browser's local extension storage, on your device. None of it is sent to us, none of it is synced, and all of it is deleted when you remove the extension.

  • Your settings. Your reading tweaks (wide column, high contrast, larger line-height) and whether the bar is shown or hidden, per site (sypCompanionState); and anything you type into the options page — an optional data URL, an optional search API key, and your Recent-list choices (sypCompanionOptions).
  • Recent — a list of what you opened or used. Shown in the bar as "Recent" and in Options as "Trail of what you opened" (sypCompanionTrail). Each entry is the name of a book, magazine, audio or video, the page you were last on, and when you first and last saw it. It never holds the text of a page. It is on by default: up to 200 entries, forgotten after 90 days, five shown in the bar — you can change all of that, or turn it off, in Options. The words you search for are kept in it only if you tick "Also remember the words I search for" (off by default), because typed words can be personal.
  • One remembered link, for up to 30 minutes. If the extension can tell you are signed out of the site, it keeps the address of the page you were on — a path on satyamyogaprasad.net and nothing else — so it can offer to take you back after you sign in (sypCompanionReturnLink). It is one slot: it is deleted when offered, when you dismiss it, or when it expires, and it is never sent anywhere. It does not read your browser's history.
  • A "what's new" marker. One date — the newest catalogue snapshot you have looked at — so the bar can tell what is new since (sypCompanionWhatsNewSeen).
  • Saved chapter lists. The chapter titles of books you have opened, so the bar does not download them again on every page turn (sypCompanionSections; no book text).
  • Reachability notes. Whether the optional local server on localhost:8010 answered recently, so the bar does not keep re-asking (sypCompanionProbe and sypCompanionCorpusProbe).

How to see and clear it

  • Recent: in the bar, "Clear trail" forgets everything in it. In Options, "Trail of what you opened" lets you show it, export it as a file, change its limits, turn it off, or press "Clear all". Turning it off stops recording but does not erase what is already kept — press Clear all for that. Unticking "remember the words I search for" and pressing Save erases the searches already kept.
  • Saved chapter lists: Options has a "Clear saved chapters" button.
  • Everything: clearing your browser's cache or history does not reach extension storage, because browsers keep it separate. Removing the extension deletes all of it.
  • Anyone who can use your browser profile can open Options and see the trail. On a shared computer, clear it.

Every network request it can make

Stated in full, because "we don't collect data" should be checkable:

  • Files inside the extension itself. The bundled page maps and stylesheets. No network involved.
  • A book's search index from syp-search.pages.dev. This is not the book. The index is a word list — which words appear on which page numbers — plus the chapter titles. It contains no sentences, no page text and no snippets, and it cannot be used to read the book. The extension downloads that one book's index, named by the book's public id, and then matches your query against it on your device. Your search words are never sent anywhere. That is also why a hosted result shows only a page number and its chapter — there is no text on the server to quote from.
  • Your own computer, if you run the companion server. If a local server is reachable on localhost:8010, searches go to it. That is your machine talking to itself; nothing leaves it.
  • A data URL you type in yourself. The options page has a blank "data base URL" field. If you fill it in with a remote address, the extension will send book ids and search terms to that address, because you asked it to. Left blank — the default — this never happens.

The extension loads no remote code and runs only on satyamyogaprasad.net and localhost:8010.

Permissions, and why

  • storage — to remember the settings above.
  • activeTab — to add the bar to the page you are reading.
  • Access to satyamyogaprasad.net — the site the bar works on.

Children, and sensitive data

The extension asks you for nothing, and sends nothing to us, from anyone of any age. It never touches passwords, payment details, location or health information, and it does not read your browser's history; the Recent list above is only what you opened inside this extension's own pages, kept on your device. Its Firefox manifest declares data_collection_permissions: none, which Firefox shows you before you install.

Changes, and how to ask

If this ever changes, this page changes with it and the date above moves. Questions or doubts are welcome — and are public, which is the point: open an issue.
